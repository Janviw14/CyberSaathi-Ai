OFFICIAL HELP • INDIA

Report financial cyber fraud

If money has been lost or a suspicious transaction has occurred, act immediately. Contact your bank/payment provider and report through official government channels.

FINANCIAL CYBER FRAUD HELPLINE1930

Call as soon as possible if money has been debited or transferred fraudulently.

Call 1930
WHAT TO DO NOW

Step-by-step reporting guide

  1. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately

    Use the official number in your bank app, on your card or on its verified website. Ask them to report the unauthorised transaction, block/freeze affected channels where appropriate, and raise a dispute. Get a complaint or ticket number.

  2. Call the national helpline 1930

    For financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 promptly. Keep the transaction details, amount, date/time, bank/wallet name and UTR/transaction ID ready. Follow the instructions given by the helpline.

  3. Submit the complaint on the official portal

    Visit the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and choose the financial fraud reporting option. Complete the form and save the acknowledgement/complaint number.

    Open official portal: cybercrime.gov.in ↗

  4. Preserve evidence

    Keep screenshots of messages, phone numbers, email headers, website addresses, UPI IDs, QR images, transaction receipts, UTR/reference numbers and bank complaint records. Do not edit the originals. Do not forward suspicious links to others.

  5. Follow up and report to local police if needed

    Keep the bank and cybercrime acknowledgement numbers. Follow up with the bank and the investigating authority. If advised or required, visit your local police station/cyber police station with the evidence and identity documents.

Keep these details ready

  • Transaction date, time and amount
  • UTR / transaction reference number
  • Bank, wallet or UPI app used
  • Beneficiary UPI ID or account details, if visible
  • Relevant screenshots, messages and phone numbers
  • Bank complaint/ticket number
Protect yourself: Never tell anyone your OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or password—not even someone claiming to be from the bank, police or 1930. Do not pay anyone who promises to recover your money for a fee.

This page provides general guidance, not legal advice. Use only official channels. Do not submit your banking credentials or sensitive documents to CyberSaathi AI.
